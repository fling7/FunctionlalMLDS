\section{Conclusion}

We presented a model-grounded authoring and runtime layer for embodied
multi-agent interfaces in Unity/WebXR. The approach makes the relationship
between spatial targets, agent responsibility, capabilities, runtime actions,
and validation outcomes explicit. Its purpose is not to replace interaction
design with a metamodel, but to make a user-facing agent interaction executable,
traceable, and testable across the scene and backend boundary.
