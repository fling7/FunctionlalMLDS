\section{Conclusion}

We presented an interaction-model-grounded assurance layer for embodied agents
in Unity. Each admitted request follows one pinned chain from spatial target
through agent capability and runtime action; rejection identifies the violated
relation.

All 93 assets and 186 target-specific candidates were structurally complete.
Under a shared resolver, the adapters agreed on 93 owner and 459 route
decisions and detected all nine common faults. The runtime admitted all 298
local/direct routes and rejected all 161 transitive/unreachable routes before
the stub without session mutation. Unity loaded 31 scenario contexts and 73
actions; all four placement workflows passed.

These corpus-bounded results establish only an executable assurance boundary;
whether the structure helps people design or understand spatial-agent
interactions remains a human-participant question.
