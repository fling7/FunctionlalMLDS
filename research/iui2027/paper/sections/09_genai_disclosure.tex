\section*{GenAI Usage Disclosure}

The case-study pipeline used OpenAI
\texttt{gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14} at temperature 0.2 to propose scene
semantics, agent roles, and local knowledge entries. The resulting stored
artifacts were reviewed and reused as fixed inputs; the reported structural
benchmark itself made no model or network calls. No generative model was used
to judge semantic answer quality reported in this paper.

OpenAI Codex, based on the GPT-5 model family, assisted with prototype
hardening, test and analysis code, literature-search support, and drafting and
editing this manuscript. The authors inspected and revised the resulting code,
verified cited sources and generated evidence, and remain accountable for all
claims and prose. The Stanford Agentic Reviewer was used only for editorial
feedback on submission drafts and did not supply scientific evidence or
reported measurements.
