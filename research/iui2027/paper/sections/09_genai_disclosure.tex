\section*{GenAI Usage Disclosure}

The case-study pipeline made eight successful structured-output calls to OpenAI
\texttt{gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14} at temperature 0.2 to propose two
scene-semantics artifacts, three agent-role artifacts with handoff declarations,
and three sets of local knowledge entries. The remaining scene-semantics
artifact was recovered deterministically without a model call.
The prompts, returned JSON, model identifiers, token metadata, and validation
records are stored with the case artifacts. No repair call was needed for the
fixed outputs used in the evaluation; the API did not expose a sampling seed.
The authors reviewed these artifacts and then froze them as inputs. Evaluation
utterances, model assembly, fault injection, measurements, and tables are
deterministic, and the reported structural benchmark made no model or network
calls. No generative model was used as a semantic answer-quality judge.

OpenAI Codex, for which the available interface identified the GPT-5 model
family rather than a more specific deployment identifier, assisted with
prototype hardening, test and analysis code, literature-search support, and
drafting and editing this manuscript. The authors inspected and revised the
resulting code, verified cited sources and generated evidence, and remain
accountable for all claims and prose. The paperreview.ai reviewer service was
used only for editorial feedback on submission drafts and did not supply
scientific evidence or reported measurements.
