\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}

The three environments are a purposive convenience corpus, and two are
exhibition-like spaces. Every natural-corpus object has an explicit asset-level
owner. Group and zone fallback are consequently demonstrated only with
synthetic copied candidate sets. The results establish behavior for these
models, not frequencies or effects in a population of rooms.

The comparison is deliberately structural. It holds scene and routing meaning
constant through executable adapters, but it does not compare complete Unity
applications, conversational answers, or human authoring sessions. Both
conditions use validators written for this study and both detect all common
mutations; the evaluation therefore does not show that \systemname{} is
intrinsically more fault-sensitive. Counts of changed artifacts and references
describe scripted patches rather than developer time or cognitive effort.

Structural grounding is necessary but not sufficient for a correct or useful
answer. We do not evaluate semantic answer quality, conversational naturalness,
user trust, workload, preference, or usability. The server validates the model,
entity, route, and evidence contract, but it cannot independently verify that a
client-reported ray-hit position came from physical geometry. A compromised
client could therefore provide plausible geometry for an otherwise valid
entity; trusted deployments require an appropriate client and transport
boundary.

The Unity evidence covers a desktop selection path and batch-mode interaction
smokes. The repository contains WebXR dependencies and a controller-ray hook,
but no physical-headset or WebXR deployment result is reported. Likewise, the
current authoring transaction supports agent placement only. Editing
responsibility, capability, and handoff assignments requires a provenance-aware
override layer and remains future work.

The version-0.5-to-V2 assembler and canonical validator are both maintained in
the same codebase. Their agreement is strong internal consistency evidence, not
independent standards conformance. The work borrows requirements, scenario,
capability, runtime, and verification concepts but does not claim full
EAST-ADL conformance.

Runtime ratios are measurements of local in-memory adapter construction and
route enumeration on one Windows machine. They exclude parsing, generation,
Unity rendering, networking, and language-model latency and should not be used
to predict end-user response time. Fault injection covers known relationship
errors and cannot establish the absence of unknown faults.

Finally, semantic case inputs were generated in earlier model calls and then
frozen. The provider does not expose a random seed for those calls, so their
semantic generation is replayable from stored responses rather than
bit-for-bit regenerable from the service. The final structural measurements are
API-free and deterministic. AI editorial feedback, including the Stanford
Agentic Reviewer, is not treated as evaluation evidence.
