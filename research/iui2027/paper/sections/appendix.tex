    \midrule

    Before constructing a model instance. \\
    Canonical V2 model &
    Referential closure, typed cardinalities, assertion/VV ownership, and no
    direct step-to-action shortcut &
    The instance is rejected before materialization. \\
    Executable profile &
    One main scenario, one provider, provider/performer/capability agreement,
    and target coherence &
    Only chains surviving every stage validator are published. \\
    Backend runtime &
    Pinned hash, trusted entity, derived group and zone, exact target/provider/
    action mapping, permitted handoff, and request/response schemas &
    Before session mutation and, when request-decidable, before a model call. \\

    Stable collider binding, explicit unresolved states, and returned
    model/target/provider/route/action agreement &
    Before serialization or answer presentation; backend checks remain
    authoritative. \\
