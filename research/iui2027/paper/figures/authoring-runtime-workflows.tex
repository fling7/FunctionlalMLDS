  \caption{Implemented authoring and visitor workflows. Placement editing is a
  revision-guarded transaction: preview does not write files; apply triggers
  deterministic regeneration and validation; accept promotes the result,
  discard and failure restore the prior bytes, and one-level undo is available
  after acceptance. In the visitor flow, a desktop selection must resolve to
  one stable entity and one permitted route before the deictic chat action;
  invalid, ambiguous, or disallowed states are blocked and observations produce
  structured assertion verdicts.}
  \Description{Two workflow diagrams. The authoring workflow starts by
  inspecting a content-hashed revision, previews structured before-and-after
  placement values without writing files, and applies a change through
  deterministic regeneration and validation. A valid working copy waits for
  an explicit decision. Accept creates an accepted revision from which one
  level of undo can restore the prior accepted revision. Discard restores the
  pre-apply byte snapshot, and regeneration or validation failure rolls back
  automatically. Commit is blocked while a decision is pending, revision
  conflicts reject apply, and free text is not treated as a model edit. The
  visitor workflow begins with a desktop camera-ray selection and classifies it
  as one target, no target, or ambiguous. A single target proceeds to backend
  validation and responsibility routing, then to the deictic chat action and
  assertion evaluation. Ambiguous or invalid selections are blocked before
  chat; unresolved responsibility or a disallowed handoff rejects routing. A
  contradiction yields fail, a missing observation yields inconclusive, and a
  successful observation yields pass. No target permits only an explicitly
  non-deictic request outside the depicted spatial flow.}
